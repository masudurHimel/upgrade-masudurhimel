Two rules for **review sessions** in the teach program (set 2026-07-22, apply from the next session onward — not retroactive to the 07-22 review):

1. **Bucket + re-teach on miss.** Whenever Masudur **skips ("pass"/"pass on") or gets a review question wrong**, give a **short re-teach** immediately, then add that item to the **carry-over bucket** in `PROGRESS.md`. Do NOT move on to new material. He must **re-teach-back every bucketed item without notes** before any new topic is served. Correctly-answered questions are not re-taught.
2. **More frequent reviews.** Review day is now **every 3rd learning day** (was every 5th).

**Why:** he wants misses to actually stick before advancing, and wants tighter spaced repetition.

**How to apply:** encoded in the teach `SKILL.md` (§Review-day rule + step 2 frequency). Ties to [[learning-program]] and [[explicit-topic-teach]] carry-over mechanics.
