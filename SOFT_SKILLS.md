# 🧠 Leadership & Influence Track (curated)

> You said you don't expect to *learn* soft skills from me — fair. So this is what I **can** do well:

> Staff is ~60% judgment & influence. Reading 10 min/week here compounds hard.

## The one idea to internalize
Senior = "I solve the problem I'm given, well." **Staff = "I figure out the right problem, get others aligned, and make the org's decisions better — often without writing the code myself."** Influence > output.

---

## Core reading (canonical, mostly free)

**Books (the two that matter most):**
- **"The Staff Engineer's Path" — Will Larson / Tanya Reilly** — *the* reference for this transition. Read 1 chapter/week.
- **"An Elegant Puzzle" — Will Larson** — eng org/systems thinking.

**Free, essential:**
- **StaffEng.com** — real staff-engineer stories + the 4 archetypes (Tech Lead, Architect, Solver, Right Hand). Read which archetype fits you.
- **lethain.com (Will Larson's blog)** — "Work on what matters", "Writing engineering strategy", scope & influence.
- **The Pragmatic Engineer (Gergely Orosz)** — newsletter; eng career + real industry practice.
- **charity.wtf (Charity Majors)** — "The Engineer/Manager Pendulum", ownership, operational maturity.

- I'll nudge you to convert strong learnings into a shareable internal note — that's how staff visibility is built.
